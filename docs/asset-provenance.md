## Sound

There is no audio file in this repository. The paper-shuffle cue is synthesized at runtime in `src/lib/audio.ts`: a short buffer of decaying white noise through a bandpass filter at 1800 Hz, 180 ms, built with the Web Audio API. It is original, it is code, and it is covered by the MIT license.

Sound is off by default. `soundEnabled` starts `false` in `createDefaultState()` and the header toggle turns it on.

This is the only sound the app makes today. A homepage music loop is a separate, unbuilt thing, tracked under audio and video assets below.

## Audio and video assets

Two different things live under this heading and merging them would be a mistake. The submission video stays outside the repository and goes to YouTube. The homepage loop, if the public demo ever loads one, is a repository asset with its own provenance row.

### The `-paid` rule for ElevenLabs audio

Owner rule, scoped to ElevenLabs-generated audio. Nothing else.

**Only an ElevenLabs file whose name contains `-paid` may be used in anything public.** Public means the repository, the live site, the YouTube video, and the Devpost entry. An ElevenLabs file without `-paid` in its name is ineligible and must not be published, committed, uploaded, or linked, not even as an excerpt.

The marker means paid plan. A name without it is older free-plan output, which is not submission-safe. Free-plan output stays unsafe when credited. Attribution is a condition on use, not a grant of commercial rights, so crediting the generator does not clear the file.

The rule is about the filename, not the folder. Renaming a free-plan file to add `-paid` does not change what it is. The name is a label for a decision that has to be made and recorded before the name goes on.

**What this rule does not cover.** The sprites, `public/logo.jpg`, the screenshots, the source, `public/favicon.svg`, and the Grok narration are all outside it. They keep their own rows in this document, and their status has nothing to do with ElevenLabs plan tiers.

`.gitignore` is the backstop, and it is narrower than the rule. It ignores `*.mp3`, `*.wav`, `*.m4a`, and `*.ogg`, then re-includes exactly two paths:

```gitignore
!public/audio/pip-the-mug-homepage-loop-paid.mp3
!public/audio/pip-the-mug-homepage-loop-paid.ogg
```

Those two files at those two paths are the only audio the repository accepts. This is not a blanket `-paid` un-ignore, and it should not be widened into one. Any other ElevenLabs file, any other `-paid` file, and every video file stays ignored or off-repo.

Do not mistake the ignore rules for the check. Git will happily accept a free-plan track renamed to one of those two paths. The check is the provenance record here, and the filename is only a label on a decision someone already made.

Neither file is on this host. `public/audio/` does not exist.
